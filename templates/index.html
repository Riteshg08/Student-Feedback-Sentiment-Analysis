How it works

  1. Export

    Download Google Form responses as a CSV. Columns for names, emails, and timestamps are ignored.

  2. Match

    Each of the six categories is matched to the best column. If a match is unclear, you map it yourself.

  3. Classify

    Blank cells are skipped. The rest are cleaned, classified, and summarised with charts you can print or download.
